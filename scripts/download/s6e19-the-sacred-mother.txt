CHAPTER 1
The Weight of Dawn

Soft morning light came through the blinds in warm stripes across the green-and-white striped wallpaper. Construction dust already hung faintly in the air from work starting downstairs, though the bedroom stayed a pocket of quiet against the early noise outside.

Mr. Gordo sat propped against the oval mirror of Buffy’s white vanity, beside the little drawers where she kept her makeup and hair ties. The closet door stood slightly open on leather jackets and pastel tops.

Lucian and Buffy slept nude under the thick white comforter pulled to their shoulders, Buffy the big spoon, wrapped around him from behind, her face tucked near his shoulder.

They began to stir. Lucian rolled onto his back to face her. Buffy’s eyes fluttered open, and before she could fully wake, Lucian gently cupped her jaw and leaned in to kiss her.

Buffy said, her voice thick with sleep, "Wait, I haven’t brushed my tee—"

Lucian kissed her anyway. The kiss was slow, deep, and full of quiet safety. Buffy melted into it, her hands sliding up to trace his shoulders and arms. The comforter shifted with them.

Two soft, polite knocks sounded on the bedroom door.

They slowly pulled apart, foreheads resting together, both smiling toward the door, their breathing still slow and heavy with sleep.

The doorknob clicked, and the door creaked open.

Tara stepped into the doorway in a flowing deep burgundy bohemian skirt and cream peasant blouse. The moment she registered they were in bed together, her face flushed pink. She immediately looked down at the floor, refusing eye contact, and gently closed the door behind her.

Tara said, "Um… good morning. I’m s-so sorry to interrupt, but Buffy, I was wondering if maybe later this afternoon you might have some time? Anya left a huge manifest and I… I really need some muscle to help move the rest of the Magic Box supplies."

Buffy said warmly, still half-tangled with Lucian, "Yeah, Tara. Give me a couple hours and I’ll come do some heavy lifting."

Tara said, still staring at the floor, nodding quickly, "O-okay. Thank you. Take your time."

Tara turned to leave.

Three brisk knocks rattled the bedroom door from the hallway.

Before she could touch the knob, the door swung open from the outside.

Willow breezed straight past Tara into the room in baggy olive-green corduroy overalls over a bright yellow ribbed sweater. Unlike Tara, she looked directly at the bed with zero embarrassment, completely unfazed.

Willow spoke rapidly, all business.

Willow said, "Hey. Quick heads-up. The construction guys Xander hired to fix the front door and windows are here. They’re gonna start drilling and sawing in like ten minutes, so it’s gonna be loud as hell all day. If you were planning on sleeping in, you might wanna grab earplugs."

Buffy slowly pulled her arms back and propped herself up on one elbow, resting her chin in her hand on top of Lucian’s chest. She stared deadpan straight ahead for a long beat — pure exhausted defeat — before turning her head back toward Willow and Tara.

Buffy said in a flat, resigned voice, "Is everyone gonna come in here today? Should I start charging admission, or do you want me to just move the coffee maker into the closet?"

Willow blinked, completely missing the sarcasm. Tara’s face somehow turned even redder.

Something thumped hard against the outside of the house, a heavy metallic clang just outside the window as an aluminum extension ladder slammed against the siding.

Willow pointed casually and said, "Oh. Yep. There they are."

A bright yellow hard hat slowly rose into view through the blinds, and Buffy watched it with growing horror.

Buffy said, deadpan, "You know what? Fine. It’s an open house. I’ll set up a continental in my bed."

Buffy threw the comforter off and stood up, reaching for her clothes, and for two seconds she stood completely naked in front of the window, a bare silhouette behind the blinds. Then she yanked on a t-shirt and hopped into jeans with her back to Tara and Willow.

Outside the window, the construction worker glanced through the blinds, and his eyes went wide.

The construction worker’s muffled, panicked voice came from outside.

The construction worker said, "Ay, caramba!"

There was a loud metallic scramble, then a heavy thud and clattering tools as the worker fell backward off the ladder into the bushes.

Tara sighed, mortified, still staring at the floor.

Tara said, "I’ll get the first aid kit."

Willow said cheerfully, watching Buffy finish zipping her jeans, "At least the house will be fixed… again. Right?"

Buffy turned around fully clothed, looking thoroughly done with the morning.

CHAPTER 2
The Try-Not-To-Laugh Champion

Later, in the upstairs bathroom, the vanity lights flickered with the heavy vibrations shaking the house from the construction downstairs. The high-pitched whine of power saws and the rhythmic hammering of a drill came through the walls, and plaster dust sifted down from the ceiling in small clouds.

Lucian stood at the mirror, dressed for the day in a crisp grey dress shirt with sleeves rolled up, black tie, and black slacks — silver cross earring catching the light, jet-black hair tied back neat enough for the day. He brushed his teeth with intense, stoic focus, and Buffy stepped up beside him.

She turned on the faucet, washed her hands, loaded her toothbrush with toothpaste, and started brushing right next to Lucian, both of them facing the mirror.

The construction noise was loud and constant, but Buffy ignored it completely. She glanced sideways at Lucian’s reflection. He looked far too serious — standing there in a full tie, aggressively brushing his teeth like it was a military operation while the house shook around them.

She decided to break him.

While still brushing, Buffy started making faces at him in the mirror. First she crossed her eyes and puffed her cheeks out like a blowfish.

Lucian’s bright aqua-blue eyes flicked over to her reflection, determined not to crack, and he tightened his jaw and doubled down on his serious brushing rhythm.

Buffy escalated: she stuck the handle of her toothbrush out of her mouth like a cigar, wiggled her eyebrows up and down, and flared her nostrils dramatically.

Lucian’s shoulders tensed, and he breathed heavily through his nose, fighting hard not to smile.

Buffy went for the kill. She pulled her lips all the way back, showing her teeth completely covered in white foam, and gave him a wide unhinged bug-eyed grin.

Lucian tried to hold it together.

Lucian said, his voice muffled, "Mmph—"

Then he lost it completely, bursting out laughing and spitting a spray of toothpaste foam directly onto the mirror with a sharp splat.

Buffy immediately dropped the face, victorious, and pulled her toothbrush out of her mouth.

Buffy said smugly, wiping her mouth, "And the Slayer retains her heavyweight title in the Try-Not-To-Laugh bracket. You didn’t even make it thirty seconds, stoic guy."

Lucian rested his forehead against the mirror, laughing breathlessly and shaking his head in defeat.

Someone pounded three times on the bathroom door.

Dawn’s muffled voice came through the door, impatient.

Dawn said, "Are you guys almost done in there? I have to do my hair before all this dust gets everywhere!"

Without missing a beat, Buffy shoved her toothbrush back into her mouth, filling it with fresh foam, then grabbed the doorknob and yanked the door open.

Dawn stood in the hallway holding a hairbrush, looking annoyed, in her weekend clothes.

Buffy said in a garbled voice, her mouth full of toothpaste, "Nope."

Buffy slammed the door shut right in Dawn’s face.

Buffy calmly stepped back up to the sink and resumed brushing her teeth like nothing had happened. Next to her, Lucian was still laughing quietly as he spit and rinsed his mouth.

CHAPTER 3
The Doublemeat Breakfast and the Escape

In the dining room just off the kitchen, morning sunlight fought through the windows, broken up by fine swirling dust from the construction. The aggressive high-pitched screech of a reciprocating saw ripped through the house from the living room next door, and the cups on the dining-room table rattled and clinked with each pass.

Lucian was still in the crisp grey dress shirt with the sleeves rolled and the black tie loosened, the silver raven buckle at his belt. Buffy was in worn jeans and a simple tee.

Tara and Willow sat close together on one side of the table, both holding mismatched mugs of tea. Willow had her free hand resting lightly on Tara’s thigh under the table. Dawn sat across from them, aggressively mixing a bowl of yogurt buried under a ridiculous mountain of Rice Krispies. Lucian sat at the head of the table, focused on something in his hands.

Buffy walked in from the kitchen carrying a greasy paper-wrapped Doublemeat Palace double cheeseburger in one hand and a black mug of coffee in the other. She dropped into the chair next to Lucian with a tired thud, the chair legs scraping against the floor. She unwrapped the burger with quick, almost aggressive movements, took a massive bite that puffed out her cheeks, then washed it down with a long gulp of coffee while staring blankly at nothing in particular.

Lucian was ignoring the coffee sitting in front of him. He held a sleek silver flip phone that opened like a tiny laptop onto a full QWERTY keyboard, his thumbs moving quickly and precisely across the tiny keys, his bright aqua-blue eyes on the small screen. A reply came in: the job interview was off. He snapped the phone shut with a sharp decisive clack that cut through the construction noise for a split second, then lifted his head and looked at the group.

The power saw hit an especially loud grinding crescendo. The whole table vibrated hard enough that the cups rattled loudly and a few Rice Krispies jumped off Dawn’s spoon.

Lucian didn’t flinch at the noise. He set the flip phone flat on the table and looked straight at Buffy, measured and already decided.

Lucian raised his voice over the noise, calm but clear.

Lucian said, "Hey. None of you have seen the public swimming pool before. We should go… to avoid all of this noise?"

He gestured with the closed phone toward the living room, where muffled shouting from the construction crew carried over the saw. A particularly violent vibration ran through the floor and up through everyone’s chairs.

Lucian turned his head and locked eyes directly with Dawn across the table. His expression stayed deadpan, but there was a tiny flicker of something almost playful in his eyes.

Lucian said, "They have a huge pool and waterslides. It’ll be way better than sitting here."

Dawn froze mid-bite, spoon halfway to her mouth, and her eyes went wide with sudden, explosive excitement. She dropped the spoon into her bowl with a clatter, sending a small puff of Rice Krispies into the air, and both of her hands started flapping rapidly at her sides like she couldn’t contain the energy.

She snapped her head toward Tara with a desperate wide-eyed pleading look, full teenage dramatics, silently begging, please, please, please.

Tara watched her for a beat, then smiled softly, warm and knowing, and gave Dawn one slow, gentle nod; Anya’s boxes at the Magic Box could wait until tomorrow.

Willow leaned forward, eyes lighting up with genuine interest.

Willow said, "Oh, I bet it’s actually decent. Can we go?"

Lucian nodded once.

Lucian said, simple and direct, "Yeah. Let’s get out of here for a few hours."

Dawn let out a loud, victorious cheer that somehow cut through the construction noise and jumped up from her chair so fast it rocked backward, its legs screeching against the floor, abandoning her yogurt and Rice Krispies completely.

Buffy slowly chewed her bite of burger and swallowed, then turned her head and stared, eyes half-lidded and mouth in a flat line, silently conveying that this was her life now, before turning back toward Lucian.

Buffy said with flat, sarcastic pragmatism, "If the pool doesn’t vibrate when I try to drink my coffee… then I am in."

Dawn let out another loud cheer and started heading for the door at a near-run, already pulling off her hoodie as she went.

CHAPTER 4
The Exit and the Car

It took twenty minutes of everyone thundering up and down the stairs, but they got there: swimsuits went on under shorts and t-shirts and cover-ups, Lucian traded his dress shirt and tie for a plain black t-shirt pulled on over his swim shorts, Tara packed a stack of towels into a tote, and Buffy filled the old plastic cooler with ice and cans of soda.

Outside, the midday sun was bright, and the front yard was a mess of construction equipment, scattered tools, and stacks of lumber, with dust hanging in the air and a large construction truck parked on the street with its bed open.

The group spilled out of the house — Buffy, Lucian, Dawn, Willow, and Tara — with the brisk, sun-squinting energy of people who were done with the noise and chaos inside.

As they walked across the front yard toward Lucian’s black 1981 Pontiac Firebird Trans Am Special Edition parked in the driveway, Buffy noticed something.

A construction worker was struggling near one of the trucks, trying to heave a strapped bundle of wooden beams onto a low moving cart, grunting as he failed to get the full weight up and over the edge.

Buffy stopped for half a second. Without a word, she walked over, bent her knees slightly, slid one palm flat underneath the bundle, and casually lifted the whole strapped-together pile of beams onto the cart. Wood scraped wood, and the cart’s wheels shifted half an inch under the sudden weight.

The construction worker froze. He slowly took off his hard hat, rubbed the back of his head with one hand, and just stared at Buffy with wide eyes and an open mouth.

Buffy didn’t even look at him; she just turned and kept walking toward the car like it was nothing.

Lucian watched the whole thing from a few steps away. He didn’t say anything, but there was a small private smile on his face as he followed her to the car.

The Firebird sat in the driveway, black with gold trim accents along the sides and hood, classic eighties muscle car styling, a T-top roof, gold badging, and tan seats inside.

Buffy opened the passenger door and got in while Lucian walked around to the driver’s side.

Dawn, Tara, and Willow squeezed into the back seat together, a tight fit for three, with Dawn in the middle and Tara and Willow pressed against the sides.

Dawn said under her breath, "This car was not built for three people back here…"

Willow said cheerfully, "It’s fine. We’re just… cozy."

Tara smiled softly.

Tara said, "It’s only for a little while."

Lucian started the car, and the engine rumbled to life with that classic deep V8 sound as he put it in reverse and backed out of the driveway.

The Firebird pulled away from the house and drove down Revello Drive, leaving the construction chaos behind.

CHAPTER 6
Arrival at the Pool

It was early afternoon at the public swimming pool, bright and sunny with scattered white clouds, and a light breeze moved through the parking lot.

The Firebird was parked near the back. The group climbed out, peeled off the shorts, t-shirts and cover-ups they had pulled on over their swimsuits back at the house, and tossed them in a heap onto the back seat, while car doors thumped shut against the distant laughter and splashing from the pool area.

Buffy stepped out of the passenger side in a deep red two-piece bikini — classic triangle top with thin straps, bottoms low on her hips with side ties. Her hair was down but slightly messy from the car ride, and she had a tired but determined look, like she was forcing herself to try and have a normal day.

Willow climbed out of the back seat in a bright teal two-piece bikini — halter neckline with a small keyhole cutout, cheeky bottoms with thin side straps. Her red hair was tied up in a messy high bun with a few loose strands framing her face. She looked quietly excited.

Tara got out last from the back seat in a soft lavender two-piece bikini — bandeau top with thin spaghetti straps and a small bow, high-waisted bottoms with moderate coverage. She had a small denim shoulder bag slung across her body. Her long dirty-blonde hair was down and slightly wavy from the humidity. She looked gentle and a little self-conscious, but there was a soft smile on her face.

Dawn stepped out in her yellow halter crop top and high-waisted yellow biker shorts. Her long brown hair was completely down, blowing across the left side of her face and shoulder from the breeze. She looked part excited, part still carrying teenage attitude.

Lucian pulled his black t-shirt off over his head beside the driver’s door and tossed it onto the seat, wearing black and white swim shorts with a bold yellow tribal pattern running down the left leg. His long jet-black hair, untied now, fell past his shoulders, and his bright aqua-blue eyes scanned the lot. The large detailed black raven tattoo spread across his chest and left shoulder, its wings wide, with the claw marks from the beach still showing through its feathers as fading pink lines. The group carried a strange mix of energy — relief at being away from the construction noise, and an underlying tiredness, especially from Buffy and Lucian. Dawn seemed the most outwardly excited. Willow and Tara quietly tried to lean into the "normal day" feeling.

They walked together along the paved walkway to the entrance. Buffy carried the cooler easily in one hand with Tara beside her, Willow and Dawn walked a little ahead, and Lucian stayed near the back, quietly observing everyone.

They reached the entrance barrier. Tara stepped forward first, opened her small denim shoulder bag, and pulled out her card. She swiped it smoothly over the scanner, and the barrier made a soft electronic beep and slid open.

The group walked through one by one.

Inside, strong early afternoon sunlight reflected brightly off the water. The pool was large and well-kept, with a deeper lap section marked by clear black lane lines on the left and a shallower recreational area on the right. Kids splashed, teenagers lounged, parents watched, and a few older people swam laps. There were lifeguard towers at both ends and a small concession stand near the back fence, with palm trees and shaded cabana-style areas along the perimeter. The deck smelled faintly of chlorine and sunscreen.

The group stepped fully into the pool area and paused for a moment, taking it all in. Dawn’s eyes widened slightly, Willow smiled, and Tara glanced at Buffy with a soft, encouraging look, while Lucian stayed close to Buffy’s side, quietly watching her reaction.

For a brief moment, the weight everyone had been carrying felt a little lighter, even if it was only temporary.

CHAPTER 7
The Shoulder Challenge

In the shallow end, bright sunlight hit the water, throwing shifting golden ripples across wet skin and the surface of the pool.

Buffy and Lucian stood chest-deep in the shallow end, watching the ongoing shoulder-standing matches. A loose crowd of maybe fifteen people had gathered along the edge, cheering and laughing.

They stood close together, her shoulder almost brushing his, and they’d been watching for a minute or two when Buffy slowly turned her head and looked up at Lucian with that very specific look — eyebrows slightly raised, eyes bright with challenge, the corner of her mouth pulled into a cocky little half-smile.

Lucian met her eyes, studied her face for a second, and gave her a small knowing nod.

Lucian took a deep breath and dropped straight down beneath the surface. The water closed over his head with a soft glunk. Bubbles rose as he moved between Buffy’s legs and positioned himself. His hands found her thighs under the water — firm and deliberate, fingers spreading wide across her skin for grip.

Lucian rose up beneath her, water sheeting off his shoulders and the raven tattoo as he stood, its wings dark against his wet skin, and Buffy was lifted cleanly out of the water and onto his shoulders in one smooth motion. Heavy streams of water poured down her body and hit the pool in bright arcs.

Buffy settled on his shoulders with a short laugh, grabbing onto his wet hair for balance. Lucian’s hands locked around her thighs, thumbs pressing into the muscle, and his aqua-blue eyes narrowed with effort as he found his footing on the slick pool floor, his long wet hair clinging to the back of his neck and shoulders. He started wading forward toward the center of the shallow end, breathing already a little heavier from the effort.

The crowd noticed them approaching and got louder, and the current competing couple turned to face them. The guy was noticeably larger than Lucian, with broader shoulders and a thicker build, and he grinned when he saw them.

The larger guy said, "You two sure about this?"

Buffy said, deadpan but clearly competitive, "We’re sure."

The two couples squared up, the blonde girl on the larger guy’s shoulders reached out and grabbed Buffy’s hands, and the match began.

Buffy was clearly stronger and more balanced, but she was consciously holding back, using controlled strength and technique instead of raw power. The blonde girl was giving it everything she had, face tight with effort, but she was struggling. Water splashed up between their locked hands with every shove.

Below, Lucian was working hard, his legs shifting and planting in the water as he fought to stay upright, his breathing getting heavier. Every time Buffy moved or leaned to counter a push, his balance adjusted, and water sloshed around his chest with every step and shift.

For a moment they actually started winning, and the crowd got louder. The blonde girl was clearly tiring, her pushes getting sloppier, and Buffy felt it; she could end this in seconds if she wanted to.

But she didn’t. She kept it fair instead, holding her strength back to a normal girl’s and letting the blonde push and push.

Then Lucian’s right foot slipped on the smooth pool floor.

He tried to recover, but with Buffy’s weight on his shoulders and the larger guy’s steady stance across from him, he couldn’t quite get his balance back. His left leg slid, and for a split second his whole body tilted. Buffy felt it and tried to compensate, but it was too late.

They went down together in a big chaotic splash.

Water exploded upward as Lucian lost his footing completely and Buffy tumbled forward off his shoulders, airborne for a brief moment before crashing into the water with him, and the splash was big enough that a few people on the edge of the pool got lightly sprayed.

They came up a second later, both laughing. Lucian was coughing a little from the water he had swallowed, pushing his wet hair out of his face. Buffy surfaced right next to him, mascara slightly smudged, still grinning, and she reached over and gave his shoulder a light, playful shove.

Dawn’s voice carried from the pool edge, teasing and loud, her hand cupped near her mouth like a megaphone.

Dawn said, "Buffy! Your base is defective! I thought you were supposed to be the strong one in the relationship!"

Dawn stood at the edge of the pool a few feet away, grinning wide, clearly enjoying this way too much. Willow was next to her, trying and failing not to laugh, and Tara just smiled softly, shaking her head.

Buffy called back, still laughing, wiping water from her eyes.

Buffy said, "Shut up, Dawn!"

Lucian was still catching his breath, but he was smiling, a real, open smile. He looked at Buffy in the water next to him, both of them soaked and dripping, and let out a short breathless laugh. Buffy looked back at him, still grinning, and for a second they just stayed there in the shallow water, the match already forgotten.

CHAPTER 8
The Edge

By late afternoon, the sunlight had softened and dropped lower, long golden rays stretching across the water and the concrete deck while a few scattered clouds drifted overhead. It was calm but not quite peaceful; an undercurrent of tiredness still ran beneath the surface.

The group sat in a loose line along the edge of the pool with their legs in the water — Buffy, Lucian, Willow, and Tara. Dawn was in the shallow end just in front of them, standing waist-deep and looking up at everyone. The water was calm here, small ripples moving gently around their legs.

Tara slowly lifted her right leg out of the water and lowered it back down in a lazy repetitive motion. After a few times, she turned her head slightly toward Willow with a small mischievous smile. She extended her toes and lightly brushed them against Willow’s toes under the water, playing with them in a quiet intimate way. Willow glanced at her and smiled softly, but didn’t say anything.

Lucian sat next to Buffy, and he’d been quiet for the last minute or so. He turned his head and looked at her directly, his expression serious, and reached over to take her hand, palm up, his fingers wrapping around hers in a deliberate gesture.

Buffy registered the shift in his energy at once, and a small flicker of tension moved through her brow and eyes, the look of someone who already knew whatever was coming wasn’t going to be light.

She let him take it anyway, her fingers closing around his without hesitation, even as her shoulders stayed a fraction too high.

Lucian placed his other hand on top of hers, sandwiching it between both of his. He met her eyes, his own bright aqua-blue and open, held them, and cleared his throat quietly.

The entire group went still.

Willow stopped moving her feet, Tara’s playful expression faded, and Dawn looked up from the water, sensing the sudden change in atmosphere. All three girls fell silent, watching Lucian and Buffy.

Lucian said, quiet and steady, though it was clearly difficult for him, "I am sorry to say… because of Calax and I.R.I.S. being destroyed last week… I am having a hard time finding a job. I am really scared, Buffy. About money. Keeping my place to stay. My life outside of yours. I know a man should not tell his woman these things. But I consider you more than a mate. More than a girlfriend. I — unlike many other people — do not keep secrets from the people I care about. That always goes horribly wrong."

He took a slow, deep breath, still holding her hand and looking straight into her eyes.

Lucian said, "I do not know what I am going to do."

Buffy didn’t answer right away.

Her chest rose with a slow breath, slightly deeper than normal, the kind someone took when they were trying to keep their reaction steady. Her shoulders stayed relaxed on the surface, but there was a visible tension in her jaw for half a second before she consciously loosened it.

She looked at Lucian for a long moment. Her eyes searched his face, not panicked but focused. There was a flash of something raw behind them, the briefest flicker of old fear: the fear of losing stability, of watching someone she cared about struggle the way she had struggled before. It was gone almost as fast as it appeared.

Her thumb slowly moved across the back of his hand — once, twice — a small grounding motion.

When she finally spoke, her voice was low, a little rough around the edges from the day, but steady. There was no performance in it, just honesty.

Buffy said quietly, with a tired kind of softness, "Okay… first of all — look at me."

She waited until he was fully meeting her eyes again. Her free hand came up and rested lightly on top of both of his, so now all three of their hands were stacked together.

Buffy said, "You don’t have to be the guy who has it all figured out before you talk to me. That’s not how this works. Not with us."

She took another slow breath, and her voice dropped even lower, almost private, even though the others were right there.

Buffy said, "I know what it’s like to feel like everything is slipping and you’re the only one holding the rope. I’ve been there. More than once. And I’m not gonna let you stand there alone trying to figure it out by yourself. Not after everything."

Her eyes stayed locked on his. There was no pity in them, just a quiet, fierce steadiness.

Buffy said, "We’ll figure something out. I don’t know what yet. But we will. And until we do… you’re not losing your place. You’re not losing anything. Not while I’m breathing."

She gave his hands one firm, deliberate squeeze. Her breathing had evened out now, and the initial flash of fear had settled into something calmer and more determined.

Buffy said, softer, almost a murmur, "You brought me back, Lucian. Piece by piece. You don’t get to fall apart alone. That’s not how this works anymore."

She didn’t look away from him or check the others’ reactions. For these few seconds, it was just the two of them.

CHAPTER 9
The Confession

The light had softened further, and the pool area was quieter now, most of the earlier crowd thinned out.

The group was still sitting at the edge of the pool, and no one had moved much since Lucian spoke. Nobody reached for a towel or the cooler; they all sat on in the low sun, their legs moving slowly in the water, still holding what Lucian had said.

Buffy was still holding Lucian’s hands, her thumb continuing its slow, steady movement across his skin; she hadn’t let go.

Willow leaned in close to Tara, her lips nearly brushing Tara’s ear as she whispered something quiet and quick. Tara listened, her eyes still on Lucian. After a moment, she nodded slowly. Willow pulled back just enough to look at her, then reached down and gently took Tara’s hand under the water, lacing their fingers together. She gave Tara’s hand a soft supportive squeeze and nodded once, encouraging her.

Tara took a shaky breath, her eyes glassy, but she forced herself to speak anyway, her voice coming out quiet and a little uneven as she stuttered, trying to push the words out from somewhere deep.

Tara said, her voice thick with emotion, "Lucian… I… we’ve all seen what you’ve done. W-with Buffy. You… you did something we never thought was possible. You brought life back into her.
