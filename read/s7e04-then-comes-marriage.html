Then Comes Marriage

Buffy the Vampire Slayer. Season seven, episode 04. Then Comes Marriage.

CHAPTER 1 Kill Tara

Out in a wooded field, moonlight cut through the branches and laid hard shadows across uneven dirt and leaves. The night wind moved low through the trees. Distant insects ticked somewhere beyond the clearing. Thirteen dead women lay arranged in a deliberate circle on the ground — bodies placed with care, not chaos.

In the center of the circle, Caleb knelt.

He wore a matte charcoal suit jacket and trousers with a white shirt open at the collar, the knees already heavily dirtied from the ground. His short dark brown hair had a clean side part. His hands were pressed palm-to-palm in front of his chest. His face was calm in a trance-like peace, and his eyes were completely black — full sclera and iris, void-like, catching almost no light.

A low hum rose under the night wind, a cold blue light gathered behind a tree at the edge of the circle, and something stepped out of it wearing Buffy’s face.

The First, as Buffy, stood by the trunk with her arms crossed as if leaning on it, in the white sweater and grey slacks Buffy had died in, her blonde hair tousled naturally. She was an exact visual match, but the eyes were colder, more calculating. A small knowing half-smile sat on her mouth. Her shoulder hung just shy of the bark without ever touching it, and no leaf stirred under her shoes when she started to walk.

She said, "I have another job for you."

She walked slowly around the outer edge of the circle while she spoke. Dirt did not compress under her steps. The wind did not catch the loose knit of her sweater the way it should.

She said, "The Trio… Willow took care of them. She crushed two of those boys in their own cables and tore the third one’s heart out of his chest with her bare hand. Then she let a little bramble eat the memory, climbed into bed beside Tara, and smiled in her sleep."

Caleb stayed perfectly still in the center of the circle, his palms still pressed together in front of his chest.

She said, "She did it for Tara. Every drop. And now she thinks she gets to walk away clean. She doesn’t. The wicked pay, preacher, and you are the instrument. Righteous punishment, by your hand."

She said, "Kill Tara."

Caleb stayed still a moment longer, then slowly lowered his hands. He reached down and wrote the name Tara in the dirt with one finger, then rose to his feet, the fabric pulling tight at his knees and the dirt staying ground into his trousers. He turned and walked away from the circle toward a truck parked nearby without looking back — steady, methodical steps on uneven ground.

The First watched him go, still smiling that small cold smile that Buffy never wore, until the blue light guttered, the hum dropped to silence, and the circle held only the dead again.

CHAPTER 2 Stamen and Pistil

At the Summers house, morning light came through the windows, birds sang somewhere outside, and the old house creaked faintly as it warmed. Upstairs, in the laundry closet off the hallway, the washing machine hummed as it started a cycle.

Tara stood at the open laundry closet loading clothes into the machine, her long golden dirty-blonde hair soft and loose, in a comfortable casual sweater and pants, her movements calm, focused, and practiced, the fabric of her sleeves shifting as she reached in and out of the drum.

From the bedroom down the hall, Willow’s voice carried out to her.

Willow said, calling from the bedroom, "Hey, have you seen my black boots?"

Tara said, "They’re by the front door. You left them there last night."

Willow appeared in their bedroom doorway down the hall, pulling a red long-sleeve sweater down over her head — black bra visible for a brief second underneath before the sweater settled. Her red hair was slightly tousled from sleep, and she wore dark jeans.

Willow said, "Thanks. I’m heading out soon. You need anything while I’m gone?"

Downstairs, Buffy and Dawn sat on the carpet with a textbook open between them. Buffy wore dark jeans and a fitted dark top, her shoulder-length layered blonde hair falling in a natural wave. Dawn was in casual teen clothes — jeans, a simple top, sneakers, hair down. Buffy was slightly struggling with the page but making an effort to be present.

Buffy said, "No, Dawn, this is the stamen… that’s the pistil. I think…"

She paused, grabbed the book, and squinted at the page.

Buffy said, "…Yeah. That’s the pistil."

Dawn gave her a small, amused smile but didn’t say anything.

CHAPTER 3 Something Worth Waking Up For

In the kitchen, the smell of Belgian waffles, vanilla, honey, and coffee hung in the air. Something sizzled softly, the coffee maker gurgled, and plates sat waiting.

Lucian finished the last of the waffles and turned toward the stairs. His long thick jet-black hair fell past his shoulders, loose, one side tucked behind his ear. He wore a black shirt with dark embroidered collar and cuffs, sleeves pushed up, dark jeans sitting low on his hips, and a simple black belt. He had a lean-muscular build; the large detailed black raven tattoo on his left shoulder and pec showed only faintly at the collar when the shirt shifted. His hands were steady. There was still a quiet residual tenderness at his lower left side — the Bringer’s knife from the theater parking lot, almost closed now, but not forgotten. He moved carefully around the kitchen without advertising it.

Lucian said, calling toward the stairs, "Breakfast is ready!"

A few moments later Willow, Tara, Buffy, and Dawn came down and took their places around the dining-room table, where Lucian had already carried everything through from the kitchen and laid it out: waffles, scrambled eggs, sausage, orange juice, and coffee.

Buffy grabbed the syrup bottle and poured a generous amount over her waffles, natural morning messiness still in her hair, and the look on her face made it plain she meant every bite.

Buffy said, "Finally, something worth waking up for."

She started eating without hesitation.

CHAPTER 4 Will You Marry Me?

At the same table, over the same breakfast, Lucian suddenly stood up from his seat with his hands behind his back.

Every head at the table turned toward him.

His long black hair moved when he stood. He looked serious and steady, shoulders slightly forward, breathing controlled.

Lucian said, "Buffy Anne Summers…"

He spoke calmly but with clear emotion.

Lucian said, "You keep telling me what I brought into your life. That I made you feel alive again. That I pulled you out of the grave. That I gave you laughter and warmth and the ability to feel something other than numbness. But you never let me finish when I try to tell you what you gave me."

Lucian said, "You showed me that loyalty still exists in this world. Real, stubborn, bone-deep loyalty. The kind that doesn’t run when things get ugly. The kind that stays even when the person standing in front of you is covered in blood and darkness and still trying to figure out how to be human again. You showed me that love isn’t just soft. It’s brutal. It’s terrifying. It’s choosing someone even when you know they might not come home. You gave me something I stopped believing in a long time ago… that two people can actually belong to each other. Not in some pretty way. In the real way."

Lucian said, "Let me be the other half to you, making us both whole. I don’t want to spend whatever time I have left on this earth wondering what it would’ve been like to call you my wife. So I’m asking you now…"

He brought his right hand around from behind his back, holding a small black ring box.

Lucian said, "Will you marry me?"

The table went completely silent.

Tara slowly stood up, tears in her eyes, her hand covering her mouth.

Tara said, her voice clear but full of emotion, "Oh my god."

Willow stared at Buffy without moving. Dawn looked back and forth between Lucian and Buffy, clearly surprised.

Buffy was frozen, her fork still pointed down at her plate. Her lip trembled as tears started falling, and she stood up shakily, her hair falling forward.

Buffy said, voice shaking, "You have no idea what it feels like… being near you. In bed or just… existing next to you. When you touch me it’s like something warm finally gets inside me. Like I can actually feel again. And I’ve had that taken away from me so many times the second I started to believe it was real."

She threw her fork down on the table and turned around, running out of the dining room and up the stairs. A second later, her bedroom door slammed shut, the sound echoing through the house.

Everyone at the table just stared at Lucian in silence.

CHAPTER 5 Sit on the Couch and Wait

In the dining room, it was quiet. From upstairs, Buffy was crying softly — audible, not overpowering. From the living room, the antique clock ticked.

Lucian hung his head for a moment before looking at Willow and Tara. His shoulders were heavy and his breathing slower. His long hair fell forward.

Lucian said, "I apologize… I should leave."

Willow immediately stood up.

Willow said, "No. Don’t leave. I want you to sit on the couch and wait."

She started heading toward the stairs. Tara watched her with a concerned expression. Willow stopped and turned back to her.

Willow said, "I am not going to use magic on her. I promised you. Please believe me, baby. Please trust me."

Tara stared at her for a few seconds, then slowly nodded, the trust between them still being rebuilt one kept promise at a time. Willow gave her a small, grateful look before heading upstairs.

Willow reached Buffy’s bedroom door and gently pressed her forehead against it.

Willow said softly, "Buffy… please let me in."

After a moment, the door slowly opened. Willow stepped inside and closed it behind her.

CHAPTER 6 Please… God, Help Me

In her bedroom, Buffy was still crying softly, her hair messy from crying and running upstairs. Willow sat on the bed next to her and put an arm around her, fingertips careful.

After a moment, Buffy looked up at Willow.

Buffy said, "I know you set me and Lucian up that night, Will. When you started that fight with Dawn… I went outside to get away from the screaming, from the house, and he came up to me on the bench across from the house."

Willow stayed quiet, letting her continue.

Buffy said, "I didn’t get with him to make you feel better about what you did to me. I got with him because… he makes me feel good in every way I wanted. That night on the bench, just sitting next to him… I started to feel warmth inside my bones again. I took his hand and kissed the back of it. I remember breathing him in. And I walked away knowing I wanted him… but I made us both wait."

Buffy said, "That day after gardening? I already knew I wanted him."

Buffy’s voice cracked as fresh tears fell.

Buffy said, "But I’m scared, Will. I’m so scared. Please… God, help me."

Willow gently brushed Buffy’s hair back from her face with her fingertips, then traced them softly down the side of her cheek.

Willow said, "I know you’re scared. And you have every right to be. But Buffy… you’re not the same girl who was broken anymore. You’re someone who’s learning how to choose life, even when it terrifies you. And Lucian isn’t asking you to be fearless. He’s asking to stand next to you while you’re scared."

She kept gently stroking Buffy’s hair.

Willow said, "You don’t have to decide anything today. But you also don’t have to carry this alone."

CHAPTER 7 If I’m Enough

Downstairs in the living room, Lucian sat on the couch with his elbows on his knees, staring at the floor, his long black hair loose and falling forward around his face.

Tara quietly sat down next to him. After a moment, she reached over and took his right hand in both of hers, holding it gently. She was calm and grounded, and she didn’t rush to reassure him with easy answers. When Lucian finally spoke, his voice was low and raw, not angry, but hurting and insecure.

Lucian said quietly, "Every time I have to be away from her… even for a few hours… I get this feeling in my chest. Like when I come back to this house, she might not be here anymore. Like one day I’ll walk through that door and she’ll just be… gone. And I won’t have gotten to say goodbye."

He swallowed hard.

Lucian said, "I know I’m not like the rest of you. I’m not a Slayer. I’m not a witch. I’m just… human. And sometimes I wonder if that’s enough. If I’m enough for her world."

Tara was quiet for a few seconds, gently rubbing her thumb over the back of his hand. When she spoke, her voice was soft but steady.

Tara said, "I know that feeling. Every time Willow or Buffy leaves to do something dangerous, I stay here with Dawn and I just… wait. And I tell myself they’ll come back. But there’s always this part of me that knows they might not. That one day they’ll walk out that door and that’ll be the last time I see them."

She looked down at their hands for a moment before continuing.

Tara said, "Loving someone like Buffy… it’s not like loving a normal person. You don’t just worry about them getting hurt or drifting away. You have to live with the fact that they might die doing something heroic. And you have to decide if you can still love them anyway, knowing that."

She squeezed his hand gently.

Tara said, "You’re not weak for being scared, Lucian. And you’re not less than because you’re human. If anything… it takes a certain kind of strength to love her knowing what it could cost you."

CHAPTER 8 The House to Yourselves

From upstairs, Willow’s voice called down gently.

Willow said, "Tara? Dawn? Can you come up here for a bit?"

Tara and Dawn exchanged a look, then headed upstairs. Lucian remained on the couch. The only sound in the room was the steady ticking of the antique clock on the wall behind him.

Tick… tick… tick… tick…

After several minutes, the three of them came back downstairs. Tara walked over to Lucian while Willow and Dawn waited near the front door.

Tara said softly, "Buffy wants to see you upstairs, Lucian."

She gave him a small, gentle look before continuing.

Tara said, "We’re going to go to the park down the street for a while. You two should have the house to yourselves."

Willow and Dawn didn’t say anything, but they both glanced at Lucian with quiet understanding before they went out the front door. The house fell silent again.

Lucian sat there for a moment, then slowly stood up. He walked upstairs, stopped in front of Buffy’s bedroom door, and placed his hand on the handle. He took a breath before opening it.

Inside, Buffy was sitting on the edge of her bed. The window was open behind her, and the curtains moved gently with the breeze. She was looking down at the blanket, her fingers lightly gripping the fabric. After a second, she slowly lifted her head and looked at him.

She didn’t speak right away. She just patted the spot on the bed next to her.

CHAPTER 9 Yes

Lucian stepped inside and closed the door behind him. He walked over and sat down next to her on the bed. For a few seconds, neither of them spoke.

Then Buffy took a shaky breath.

Buffy said, "If you had left… I would’ve come downstairs and seen that you were gone. And I would’ve felt it. That feeling I get when good things disappear. I would’ve smelled the breakfast you made and seen your footprints in the carpet and it would’ve felt like you were already gone for good. Like I lost you before I even got to say yes."

She looked down again for a second, then back up at him. Her voice was quieter but steadier.

Buffy said, "I’m glad you stayed. I needed you to stay."

She reached over and took his hand.

Buffy said, "I love you. And the answer is yes. I want this. I want something normal with you more than anything right now. So… yes. I’ll marry you."

Lucian took the ring from the small black box and slipped it onto her finger.

Lucian’s face softened. His bright aqua-blue eyes went gentler when he looked at her. He gently reached out with his right hand to touch her face, leaning in to kiss her. Buffy turned her head slightly at the last second.

Buffy said, "I have snot face right now. I’m a mess."

Lucian didn’t pull away. He just rested his forehead against the side of her head for a second.

Lucian said softly, "Remember? I love every part of you. Even the mess."

Buffy let out a small, wet laugh against his mouth as he kissed her. The kiss deepened quickly, turning desperate. She pushed forward, pinning him back onto the bed. In one motion she pulled his shirt up and off, tossing it aside. The raven tattoo showed fully on his left shoulder and chest, wings spread upward, realistic feathers and talons. The almost-healed wound at his lower left side showed as a still-tender scar rather than open bandage. She kissed her way up his stomach, then his chest, then his neck, before finding his mouth again. Her hands gripped him tightly, like she was afraid he might disappear if she let go.

Behind them, the curtains kept stirring at the open window.

CHAPTER 10 Something That’s Just Hers

At the park, playground sounds drifted around them: kids playing, distant laughter, birds. A soft breeze moved their hair.

Willow, Tara, and Dawn sat on a bench near the playground. Dawn was between them, kicking at the grass with her sneakers.

Dawn said, "So… she actually said yes?"

Willow said, "I can’t believe it either… life changes, it evolves into something, always moving forwards."

Dawn said, "I thought she’d freak out more. Like… run away or something. But she didn’t."

Tara said gently, "She did freak out. Just not in the way we expected."

Dawn nodded slowly. She picked at a loose thread on her jeans.

Dawn said, "I’ve been kind of a brat to her a lot. Especially lately. I know I have. But… I don’t know. When she told us upstairs, she looked scared. Not the usual ‘I have to save the world’ scared. Like… real scared. About being happy."

She glanced at Willow and Tara.

Dawn said, "I think she needs this. Even if it’s crazy. Even if it’s fast. She needs something that’s just hers. Something normal. And if Lucian makes her feel like that… then I’m not gonna be a dick about it."

Willow looked at Dawn, a little surprised but clearly touched.

Willow said, "That’s really mature of you, Dawn."

Dawn shrugged, a little embarrassed, and said, "Yeah, well… don’t get used to it."

Tara smiled softly and reached over to gently squeeze Dawn’s shoulder.

Tara said, "It’s okay to be happy for her, you know. Even if it feels weird. She’s been through so much. If she wants something good… we should let her have it."

Willow said, "I agree. She’s been fighting for so long. If Lucian is what she wants, then I’m happy for her. She deserves to feel safe with someone."

Dawn was quiet again for a moment, then spoke a little quieter.

Dawn said, "Do you guys think it’ll last? Like… with everything that keeps happening to us?"

Tara and Willow exchanged a look. Tara answered first.

Tara said, "I don’t know. But I think that’s not really the point right now. She said yes because she wants it. That’s enough for me."

Willow nodded, but there was a very slight tightness in her expression that she quickly hid.

Willow said, "Yeah. She wants this. So we should support her."

Dawn leaned back on the bench and let out a long breath.

Dawn said, "Okay. Then I’m in. If she’s doing this… I’m not gonna make it harder for her."

CHAPTER 11 Still Clean

On a commercial airplane, the engines hummed low and the air circulation hissed. Seatbelt chimes sounded in the distance under the muffled noise of passengers.

Caleb sat in the middle seat. Beside him, a tired-looking woman in her late twenties held a baby. The baby was quiet, staring directly at Caleb with wide eyes.

The woman shifted in her seat, clearly needing to get up. She glanced at Caleb, noting the suit, the calm, the priest-like stillness.

The woman said, "Excuse me, Father… would you mind watching him for just a minute? I really need to use the bathroom."

Caleb turned to her with a small, polite smile.

Caleb said, "Of course, ma’am. Take your time."

The woman carefully handed the baby to him. Caleb supported the child’s head gently as she got up and walked down the aisle toward the bathroom.

Once she was gone, Caleb slowly looked down at the baby. The child continued staring up at him in silence. Caleb’s expression stayed calm, but his eyes were cold.

Caleb said quietly, speaking only to the baby, "You ain’t been touched by sin yet, have you, little one? Still clean. Still whole. Fragile little thing… but protected for now. The Lord’s still got His hand on you."

He gently brushed a finger across the baby’s cheek, his voice dropping lower.

Caleb said, "Not like them filthy girls I’m fixin’ to visit soon. They’ve been spoiled. Corrupted. They need purifying. And I aim to see it done."

The baby kept staring at him without making a sound. Caleb’s face remained disturbingly gentle.

CHAPTER 12 Sunnydale, Please

Outside LAX, the main entrance was busy. Palm trees stood along the curb. Taxis and airport shuttles lined up. Travelers with luggage moved in and out of the sliding glass doors. Late afternoon sun cast long light across the pavement. Luggage wheels rolled, airplane engines droned in the distance, car horns sounded, and people talked.

Caleb stepped out through the doors carrying a small black bag. He walked at a calm, steady pace toward the taxi line. His suit jacket and trousers were slightly rumpled from travel, and he wore black oxfords. His dark eyes caught almost no reflection. The jacket moved naturally with his stride.

He approached a yellow taxi at the front of the line. The driver, a middle-aged man, glanced at him through the open window.

Caleb gave him a small, polite smile as he opened the back door.

Caleb said in a friendly, soft Southern drawl, "Evenin’. Mind if I hop in?"

The cab driver said, "Yeah, sure. Where you headed?"

Caleb slid smoothly into the back seat, set his bag beside him, and pulled the door shut with a solid thunk. He leaned forward slightly between the front seats, still wearing that same polite, almost warm expression.

Caleb said, "Sunnydale, please."

The driver nodded and pulled away from the curb. Caleb sat back, calm and relaxed, watching the airport slowly disappear behind them through the window.

CHAPTER 13 The Other Shoe

At golden hour, the sun was just starting to set through Buffy’s open bedroom window. The sheer light-blue curtains moved gently in the evening breeze, casting soft shifting patterns across the room and the bed.

Buffy was lying on her stomach, head turned to the side on the pillow, eyes half-closed in a rare moment of peace. Sheets covered her from the chest down. Lucian sat up slightly against the headboard, the sheets resting low around his waist. His long black hair fell loosely around his shoulders. The raven tattoo was visible on his left shoulder and chest. The almost-healed scar at his lower left side had stopped troubling him. He watched her for a moment, then slowly reached out with his right hand.

He placed his index finger at the small of her back, just above the sheet line, and began to trace it slowly upward. His finger moved deliberately along her spine. Her muscles twitched and tightened in small ripples under his touch. Goosebumps spread outward across her skin as his finger continued its slow path up between her shoulder blades, over her shoulders, past the faint pink crescents the motel demon’s teeth had left near the base of her neck, almost healed now, and finally to the back of her neck. Her hair shifted slightly with the breeze from the window. Sheets moved with their bodies.

Buffy let out a soft breath through her nose, somewhere between a sigh and a quiet laugh. She didn’t open her eyes yet.

Buffy said softly, her voice a little rough, "I keep waiting for the other shoe to drop. For something to come crashing through the window or for me to wake up back in the grave. But it’s not happening. You’re still here. The house is still standing."

She finally opened her eyes and turned her head a little more toward him, a small, tired but genuine smile on her face.

Buffy said, quieter, "I don’t know how to do this part. The part where I let myself have something good without waiting for it to get ripped away."

Lucian kept his hand resting gently on the back of her neck, thumb moving in a slow, soothing motion. His voice was low, direct, but gentle.

Lucian said, "You don’t have to know how. You just have to let it happen. I’m not going anywhere."

Downstairs, the front door slammed shut — loud and unmistakable. Dawn’s voice carried up, followed by the sound of them moving into the kitchen. Their voices were muffled at first.

Tara said from downstairs, muffled, "I don’t feel like cooking tonight… it’s already so late."

Willow said, muffled at first, then louder, "Then we’re ordering pizza. Buffy! The usual?!"

Buffy said softly, half to herself, "And I can hear them downstairs arguing about pizza like the world isn’t ending every other Tuesday."

Buffy let out a small, breathy laugh against the pillow, her shoulders shaking a little with it. The sound of normal life downstairs seemed to pull her back into the moment. She lifted her head slightly and yelled back down the stairs, her voice carrying that familiar mix of exasperation and affection.

Buffy yelled downstairs, "Yes, WILL!"

She dropped her head back down onto the pillow, still smiling faintly. The light kept moving across the bed as the curtains swayed.

CHAPTER 14 May the Cycle Bring You Peace

Outside the Summers house that evening, a beat-up delivery car pulled up. A twenty-year-old blonde guy with shaggy hair and a red "Buck a Slice" shirt got out. He looked at the house for a second, visibly uneasy, then grabbed the five large pizza boxes and walked up the path.

Willow opened the front door before he could knock and took the stack of pizzas from him while Tara paid. The pizza guy mumbled a quick thanks and hurried back to his car. Willow and Tara glanced at each other and laughed quietly as they closed the front door.

In the dining room, Lucian was already sitting next to Buffy at the table. He’d changed for the evening — black long-sleeve sweater and black slacks, long hair loose around his shoulders. Buffy wore a short white dress with small earrings and light natural makeup, hair slightly tousled. Tara had pulled on a soft cardigan over a simple top. The table was set with plates, napkins, soda, and a bottle of rosé.

Everyone started grabbing slices — extra cheese, sausage, pepperoni, Hawaiian, and a veggie pizza for Tara and Willow. Boxes rustled as the pizza got served.

Once everyone had food, Buffy looked around the table. Her voice was casual but carried real feeling.

Buffy said, "I love everyone at this table… even you, squirt. And don’t you forget it."

She said it while looking at Dawn with a small smirk. Dawn rolled her eyes but smiled and didn’t argue back for once.

After a moment, Tara set her slice down and slowly stood up. She picked up her glass of rosé, a little shy but steady. The table quieted down as she raised it.

Tara said, soft, sincere, "I was thinking… life moves in cycles. It turns and changes and it never really stays still, no matter how much we might want it to sometimes. We lose things… and we come back. We go through darkness and we find light again, even when we think we won’t."

She looked at Buffy with gentle eyes, her voice warm and steady.

Tara said, "Buffy, you’ve been through so many of those cycles. More than anyone should have to. And now you’re choosing to step into a new one… with someone who wants to stand with you."

Tara glanced at Lucian with a small, genuine smile before continuing.

Tara said, "Having you here, Lucian… it feels like the house is moving forward in a good way. Like there’s more room for something better. So… to both of you. May the cycle bring you peace this time."

She took a small sip from her glass. Willow started clapping and everyone else joined in — a little awkward but full of warmth.

Buffy tried to laugh it off, but her eyes started to get glassy. She quickly looked down at her plate and blinked hard several times, trying not to ruin her makeup. She took a small bite of pizza to cover it, still smiling faintly as the moment passed and everyone went back to eating and talking.

After the clapping died down, the mood was still warm but more relaxed now.

Dawn picked at a piece of pineapple on her Hawaiian slice, then glanced between Buffy and Lucian.

Dawn said, "So… are you guys like… actually getting married married? Like, with a dress and everything? Or are you just gonna do the courthouse thing and call it a day?"

She tried to sound casual, but there was real curiosity underneath it.

Willow wiped her hands on a napkin and jumped in before anyone else could answer.

Willow said, "I mean, either way is fine! Courthouse is fast and easy, but if you wanted something small here at the house we could totally do that. Nothing crazy, just… you know, us. Some flowers. Maybe some candles. I’m just saying, it could be nice."

Tara gave Willow a soft look, then turned her attention to Buffy and Lucian with a gentle expression.

Tara said, "Whatever you two decide… it doesn’t have to be big. It just has to feel right for you."

Lucian shifted slightly in his seat. He’d been mostly quiet, just listening. He glanced down at his own side for a second, his left hand settling over the almost-healed spot under the sweater, then spoke in his usual low, steady voice.

Lucian said, "My wound is almost healed up now. Figured I should mention it. Didn’t want anyone thinking I was gonna slow anyone down if things get rough again."

Buffy turned her head toward him, a small smirk pulling at the corner of her mouth. She let out a quiet little laugh under her breath.

Buffy said, "I noticed."

She said it with a teasing tone, eyes flicking over him for half a second before she took another bite of pizza, still smiling faintly.

The table felt easy for a moment. People kept eating. The low sound of chewing and casual conversation filled the dining room.

Then the front doorbell rang.

Buffy immediately tensed, her whole body going still mid-chew, and with a large mouthful of pizza she slowly lowered the slice back onto her plate. She turned in her chair, eyes locked on the front door down the hallway. The warmth from a second ago was gone, replaced by sudden alertness.

CHAPTER 15 Came Back Wrong

In the dining room, Buffy rose from her seat at the table. She held one hand out toward the others, palm facing them.

Buffy said, "I got it."

She walked out of the dining room and down the short hallway to the front door, and opened it.

Spike was standing on the porch in his torn black coat and boots, bleached hair catching the porch light, the burn on his cheek nearly faded.

The porch light threw harsh shadows. Buffy’s face hardened the second she saw him. She stepped outside, closed the door firmly behind her, and moved in close — right inside his space. Her voice was low and furious.

Buffy said, "Spike. You can’t be here."

Spike looked at her for a second, then slowly lifted his right hand toward the side of her face. Buffy immediately turned her head away and took a step back.

Spike scoffed, a bitter half-smile on his face.

Spike said, "Things have changed since the singing, haven’t they?"

He took a small step forward, eyes locked on hers.

Buffy wrapped her arms around herself, like she was trying to hold something in. Her voice came out quieter, but firm.

Buffy said, "Spike… I don’t want you."

Spike laughed once under his breath — ugly, quiet, mean. He leaned in like a secret.

Spike said, "Don’t play clean with me, love. Your little redhead already got blood on her hands. Warren. Jonathan. Andrew. She put the Trio in the ground. Ask her. Ask her what she did when you weren’t looking."

Buffy’s face changed — shock first, then something colder trying to stand up inside it.

Buffy said, "You’re lying."

Spike said, "Am I? Why do you think they have been so quiet lately, huh? No traps, no magic, no tricks… just silence… because they are dead… and Red did it…"

Spike laughed quietly and said, "That’s the thing, you see, about your little group. Always keeping secrets from each other… that come with bite in the end… just like me…"

Spike’s face twisted harder, and he suddenly grabbed her by both arms and shook her once, hard.

Buffy reacted fast. She slapped him hard across the face with her left hand. The sound was sharp. Spike stumbled back a step.

His face changed, the yellow eyes and the ridged brow and the fangs all sliding into place at once, and he attacked.

He kicked her hard in the stomach, sending her stumbling backward. Buffy caught herself against the porch railing and slid down it to the porch boards, one hand pressed to her stomach. Spike grabbed his head with both hands, bracing for the pain of the chip firing.

Nothing happened inside his skull at all.

He slowly lowered his hands, realization dawning on his face. He looked at Buffy.

Buffy stared back at him in pure terror, one hand still pressed against her stomach where he had kicked her. She understood exactly what this meant.

The chip wasn’t working on her.

The ward was. The moment he had grabbed her, a low vibrating tone had started deep in Spike’s inner ear, and now it was swelling louder, the porch boards seeming to tilt under his boots, and he swayed with it and planted his feet wide, fighting the lurch out of sheer spite.

Inside the house, Lucian stood up from the table after a moment. He walked toward the front door, calling out casually.

Lucian said, "Buffy, your pizza is getting cold…"

He opened the front door and stepped out onto the porch.

He immediately froze when he saw Buffy on the ground and Spike standing over her in game face. Without hesitation, Lucian lunged forward and swung hard with his right fist, aiming straight for Spike’s face.

Spike ducked under the punch and countered with a vicious uppercut under Lucian’s chin. The impact was brutal. Lucian was knocked clean off his feet and crashed onto the porch. He landed hard on his back, arms spread out to his sides, dazed and barely conscious. The almost-healed wound at his side jarred with the fall. His head lolled slightly as he tried to focus.

Spike suddenly screamed and grabbed his head with both hands, the chip firing violently inside his skull. He stumbled backward and crashed against the wooden banister of the porch, still clutching his head in agony. The pain lasted for several long seconds.

Buffy saw it. The chip had torn through his skull the second he hit Lucian, and it hadn’t made a sound when he kicked her.

Slowly, Spike lowered his hands. He looked over at Buffy, who was still on the ground staring at him in horror. A slow, cruel smile spread across his face.

Spike said, "See? Chip doesn’t work when I hit you, and only you. You are a monster, a demon. Just like me."

He took a step closer, voice low and venomous.

Spike said, "You didn’t just come back… you came back wrong."

Buffy’s eyes filled with tears and started to glaze over as the words hit her. She didn’t move. She just stared at him, looking completely lost.

The front door opened again. Willow and Tara stepped out onto the porch.

Willow took one look at the scene — Buffy on the ground, Lucian dazed, and Spike standing there in vampire face. Her expression hardened. She slowly raised her right hand at her side and curled her fingers into a claw-like shape. The air around her hand began to visibly vibrate and distort. Her eyes started to glow with a faint, dangerous light from within.

Spike saw it. He took one last look at Buffy, then turned and ran — disappearing into the darkness of the front yard and vanishing between the shadows of the trees.

Willow stayed frozen in place for a moment, hand still raised, her chest rising and falling fast. The glow in her eyes slowly faded.

CHAPTER 16 Starting with the Witch

On the outskirts of Sunnydale, the taxi pulled up outside the old, rundown winery. Caleb stepped out, paid the driver without a word, and watched the car drive away into the night. He adjusted his suit jacket, picked up his small bag, and walked toward the building like a man arriving at church on Sunday.

Inside, the winery was dark and abandoned. It was dusty, full of old wooden barrels and rusted equipment under weak hanging lights. Caleb moved through the upper level without hesitation, eventually finding the stairs that led down into the basement.

When he reached the bottom, he stopped.

Sickly yellow light mixed with deep shadows. The basement felt like a church that had been desecrated. Dozens of Bringers in brown robes were kneeling in a large, perfect circle on the concrete floor. Their hooded heads were bowed low, faces empty where their eyes should have been. They didn’t move. They didn’t make a sound. They were waiting for him.

Caleb stepped into the center of the circle, his shoes echoing softly. He looked around at the kneeling figures for a long moment, then slowly removed his jacket and folded it over one arm. His voice was calm, clear, and carried the steady cadence of a sermon.

Caleb said, "Brothers… the Lord has been patient. But patience ain’t the same as mercy."

He began slowly walking the inside of the circle as he spoke, his voice low and measured.

Caleb said, "There’s a girl out there. A Slayer. She died once already… and the Lord saw fit to spit her back out. Now she walks this earth like she’s still one of His children. Like she ain’t been touched by something foul."

He stopped walking and stood still in the sickly yellow light.

Caleb said, "And beside her… there’s another. A witch. One who’s tasted power that don’t belong to her. Who’s dragged the dead back up out of the ground and called it love."

Caleb’s voice dropped even lower, almost gentle.

Caleb said, "They think they’re clean. They think they can build little lives. Little homes. Little futures. But the Lord sees the rot. He sees the corruption they carry in their skin and in their souls."

He turned slowly, looking at the Bringers surrounding him.

Caleb said, "So we’re gonna help them remember what they are. We’re gonna peel back the lies… and we’re gonna make them clean again."

He paused, then finished quietly.

Caleb said, "Starting with the witch."

Caleb stood motionless in the center of the circle. The Bringers remained perfectly still, heads bowed, waiting for his next command.
